3. Account Options

Guest use

You may use Organized without creating an account.

When you use Organized as a guest, your tasks, lists, and related content are stored locally on your device using the application's local database. This information is not stored on Organized's backend while you remain a guest.

Guest data is normally removed when the application is uninstalled and cannot be recovered by Organized.

If you later create an account, your local data may be synchronized with your Organized account.

Signed-in accounts

If you create an Organized account, your account is associated with:

  • A Google account identifier (sub);
  • Your Google account email address;
  • Your Google account display name;
  • An Organized-generated unique identifier;
  • Your account creation date; and
  • A custom username, if you choose to set one.

Your custom username can replace the Google display name used within Organized.

Organized does not receive or store your Google password.

When you sign in with Google, the Organized application obtains a Google ID token from Google's authentication services and sends that ID token to the Organized backend.

The Organized backend exchanges and verifies the ID token with Google's authentication service and receives the Google account information necessary for account authentication and account functionality, including your email address, Google account identifier (sub), and display name.

4. Information You Store

When you use Organized while signed in, Organized stores and synchronizes information that you choose to create in the application, including:

  • Tasks and their content;
  • Lists and their content;
  • Your selected application language;
  • Your custom username; and
  • Other information strictly necessary to provide synchronization and account functionality.

Your signed-in data is automatically synchronized with Organized's backend.

Synchronization cannot currently be disabled while using a signed-in account. You may choose whether synchronization uses Wi-Fi or cellular data where this option is provided by the application.

Organized does not intentionally request sensitive personal information as part of its core functionality. However, because you can create your own task and list content, you may choose to enter personal or sensitive information into the application. Organized does not use your content to infer sensitive characteristics or for behavioral profiling.

5. Technical and Operational Information

API traffic

The backend maintains limited metadata about API requests made by the application. This may include:

  • A request identifier;
  • HTTP method;
  • Requested API path;
  • HTTP response status;
  • Request duration;
  • Request size;
  • Response size;
  • The internal account identifier associated with the request, where applicable; and
  • The time at which the request was recorded.

API traffic logs do not contain API request or response bodies.

The most recent API traffic may also be included in a bug or feedback report when you explicitly submit such a report.

API traffic logs are retained for up to 90 days.

Backend monitoring and error logging

Organized uses locally hosted monitoring and logging tools, including Grafana, Alloy, and Loki, to monitor service health, API traffic, and critical backend errors.

These tools are currently hosted on the same machine as the Organized backend.

Organized does not use these monitoring systems for advertising, behavioral profiling, cross-service tracking, or analytics.

Backend logs may contain authentication-related events and technical errors, including database errors where relevant. Error messages are retained only where necessary for operational, security, troubleshooting, or service-maintenance purposes.

Organized does not intentionally include passwords, authentication secrets, or other sensitive credentials in its logs.

Organized does not intentionally collect or store IP addresses, user-agent information, or similar infrastructure-level connection information as part of its own application logging described above.

However, the hosting provider or other infrastructure used to operate the Service may independently generate or retain technical logs, such as connection information, for infrastructure operation, security, abuse prevention, or other purposes. Such processing is governed by the applicable provider's own policies and contractual arrangements.

6. Feedback and Bug Reports

You may voluntarily submit feedback, bug reports, suggestions, or other comments through Organized.

When you submit a report, the report itself is stored on Organized's backend.

To help diagnose the issue, Organized may automatically attach limited technical information available at the time of submission, including:

  • Device information;
  • Operating system information;
  • Organized application version and build;
  • Network type;
  • Relevant account information;
  • The 15 most recent API requests; and
  • The 50 most recent application log entries.

The 15 API requests contain metadata only and do not include API request or response bodies.

The 50 application log entries are the most recent entries available at the time the report is submitted. They are not independently transmitted to Organized solely for crash reporting or analytics; they are attached to a report only when you voluntarily submit such a report.

Application logs may include technical error messages.

You should avoid including sensitive personal information in a report unless it is necessary to explain the issue.

Reports are retained for up to 365 days so that Organized can investigate reported issues, identify recurring bugs and technical problems, compare related reports over time, and improve the reliability and maintenance of the Service.

7. Notifications

Organized may offer push notifications.

Push notifications are optional and require the relevant permission from your device's operating system.

When you enable push notifications, Organized may process and store a push notification token associated with your account so that notifications can be delivered to your device.

Organized uses the Expo Server SDK and Expo's push notification services for push notification delivery.

When a notification is sent, Organized sends the relevant push notification request and token to Expo's servers. Expo then forwards the notification through the applicable platform push notification service, including Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for Android devices.

The information involved in this process may include the push notification token and the notification payload necessary to deliver the notification.

Push notification tokens may be stored on Organized's backend and are encrypted. They are retained for no longer than 30 days, and stale tokens may be removed earlier.

Organized does not use push notification tokens for advertising or cross-service tracking.

11. Data Storage and Security

Organized's core backend infrastructure and database are hosted in the European Union, currently in France.

API communications between the application and Organized's backend use HTTPS.

Organized uses Google Sign-In for authentication.

Refresh tokens are stored server-side in hashed and salted form.

Push notification tokens stored by Organized are encrypted.

Organized restricts access to backend systems and operational data according to the access required to operate and maintain the service.

The primary application database is not currently encrypted at rest.

Organized uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of storing or transmitting information can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Retention

Organized retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by applicable law.

In general:

  • Account and service data is retained while your account is active and until it is deleted, subject to the exceptions described below;
  • API traffic logs are retained for up to 90 days;
  • Bug and feedback reports are retained for up to 365 days;
  • Push notification tokens are retained for up to 30 days;
  • Backup copies may retain deleted production data for up to 14 days; and
  • Other information may be retained where necessary to comply with a legal obligation, establish or defend legal claims, maintain security, prevent fraud or abuse, or otherwise where permitted by applicable law.

Retention periods may overlap. For example, deleting an account does not necessarily cause information contained in a still-valid backup, technical log, or previously submitted report to disappear immediately.

13. Account Deletion

You can delete your Organized account directly from within the application.

When you request account deletion, your account and associated service data are directly deleted from active production systems.

This includes the account information and synchronized service data associated with your account.

Certain limited information may remain temporarily or for a separate retention period where necessary, including:

  • API traffic logs, for up to 90 days;
  • Bug and feedback reports, for up to 365 days;
  • Backup copies, for up to 14 days; and
  • Push notification tokens, for up to 30 days.

Information retained for these purposes is not retained indefinitely and is deleted according to the applicable retention period.

Where information must be retained because of a legal obligation or another legally permitted reason, it will be retained only for the period necessary for that purpose.

  • Right to rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to erasure: You may request deletion of your personal data where the applicable legal conditions are met.
  • Right to restriction: You may request that certain processing be restricted where the applicable legal conditions are met.

  • Right to data portability: Where applicable, you may request certain personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format and request its transmission to another controller where technically feasible.
